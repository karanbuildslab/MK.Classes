About M.K Classes

Education with concepts, discipline and personal attention.

M.K Classes was established in 2021 under the guidance of Mr. Manish Prajapati. Our aim is to create a learning environment where every student receives proper guidance and understands concepts instead of simply memorising them.


We provide education from Nursery to 12th, along with drawing competitions, social activities, educational picnics, practical explanations and celebrations that encourage students to learn beyond textbooks.
