About M.K Classes

Education With A Purpose

M.K Classes is focused on building strong academic foundations and helping students understand concepts instead of simply memorising them.

Our learning environment combines experienced teachers, small batches, regular tests, practical explanation, planned study material and individual attention.

Along with academics, students get opportunities to participate in cultural celebrations, sports, competitions, educational picnics and creative activities.

  • Concept-focused teaching
  • Regular tests and revision
  • Individual student attention
